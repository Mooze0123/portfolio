Moto Guzzi VC 6.

A robot vacuum cleaner shaped by the visual language of a century-old motorcycle brand.

From imageboard to clay, Alias, workshop and final appearance model.

Moto Guzzi VC 6 · workbook case studyStart the journey ↓

About the project

A new category with a very established spirit.

This project was part of the master course “Advanced Prototyping” at Luleå University of Technology. Our brief was to design and build an advanced prototype for a brand outside its usual category.

We chose Moto Guzzi and translated its racing history, exposed mechanics and restrained Italian styling into the VC 6: a compact robot vacuum that still feels robust, crafted and unmistakably Guzzi.

Role
Industrial design, prototyping and visual development
Team
Six designers
Duration
Three months · spring 2023
Methods
Research, clay, 3D scanning, Alias, CNC, 3D printing, vacuum forming and laser cutting

01 / The process

It is about the journey.

The project moved through a deliberate sequence: understand the brand, open up the form space, make it physical, then build the details that make the object feel real.

02 / Analysis

Pure Italian spirit.

We looked at Moto Guzzi’s history, current models and future direction. Passion, workmanship, innovation and tradition became a set of design principles rather than decoration.

03 / Ideation

Many sketches. Three directions.

Three groups explored sporty, traditional and brand-wide interpretations. The strongest ideas became the starting points for the clay workshop.

04 / Clay modeling

From sketch to clay.

Clay gave the team a way to test light, shadow, scale and touch before committing to a digital surface. It made the abstract brand cues measurable in three dimensions.

05 / 3D scanning and 3D modeling

A physical idea, made editable.

Once the concept was chosen, the clay model was scanned and rebuilt in Autodesk Alias. The digital model became the shared language for rendering, manufacturing and team decisions.

06 / Manufacturing

Turning surfaces into parts.

The prototype combined CNC-milled foam, vacuum-formed covers, 3D-printed details and laser-cut graphics. Each process was chosen for the character of the part it needed to make.

07 / The product

VC 6 — a new product with an old soul.

The final prototype carries the hexagon, tank-like curvature, exposed details and matte colour blocking of the V7 Stone line into a compact domestic object.

Designing for a new category without losing what makes a brand recognizable.

The VC 6 is the result: a product that borrows Moto Guzzi’s attitude rather than simply copying its shapes.
